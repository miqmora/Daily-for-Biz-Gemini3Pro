# PRODUCT DEFINITION: Dailymotion for Business (Hybrid Visual Identity)

## 1. Strategic Vision
**The Core Concept: "The Convergence"**
We are not just merging two business units; we are merging Human Intuition with Machine Intelligence. The new Dailymotion for Business identity sits at the intersection of:
*   **Video (The Medium):** Fluid, cinematic, immersive.
*   **Tech (The Engine):** Precise, data-driven, powerful.
*   **Human (The Purpose):** Emotional, accessible, partner-centric.

**The Goal:**
To create a digital experience that feels "Sovereign" (Control/Power) yet "Transparent" (Clear/Honest). The user should feel they are wielding a powerful tool that is easy to master.

## 2. Design System: "Pulse UI"
Our design language is living and breathing. It doesn't just sit there; it reacts, pulses, and flows.

### Core Principles
1.  **Fluid Layouts:** No rigid boxes. Content flows into containers. Transitions are seamless (using Framer Motion `layout` animations).
2.  **Cinematic Interactions:** Scroll-triggered video reveals, parallax data layers, and "slow-motion" hover states that mimic high-end video editing software.
3.  **Data Precision:** When we show numbers or graphs, they are razor-sharp lines (1px) contrasting with soft video backgrounds.
4.  **The "Glow" vs. "Warmth":**
    *   **Tech Actions** (e.g., "Analyze"): Electric Blue Glow.
    *   **Human Actions** (e.g., "Contact Us"): Warm Coral/Soft White.

## 3. Visual Identity (Colors & Typography)

### Color Palette
*   **Canvas (Backgrounds):**
    *   `Rich Black` (#050505): The void where creativity happens. Deep and premium.
    *   `Charcoal` (#121212): Secondary surfaces/cards.
*   **Primary Accents:**
    *   `Electric Blue` (#3B82F6): Represents Technology, AI, Data. Used for active states, primary buttons, and data lines.
    *   `Warm Coral` (#F43F5E): Represents Humanity, Emotion, Partnership. Used for storytelling elements, testimonials, and secondary calls to action.
*   **Neutral:**
    *   `Mist White` (#F3F4F6): Primary text. High legibility.
    *   `Steel Gray` (#9CA3AF): Secondary text.

### Typography
*   **Headings:** `Inter` (Tight tracking, bold weights). Direct, modern, no-nonsense.
*   **Body/UI:** `Inter` (Regular/Medium). Clean and readable.
*   **Data/Technical:** `Space Grotesk` (Monospace feel). Used for numbers, labels, and "tech" moments.

## 4. Content Strategy & Tone
*   **Voice:** "The Savvy Partner." We don't shout; we explain with confidence.
*   **Keywords:** Business Outcome, Sovereign Tech, Transparency, Video Expert.
*   **Microcopy Rules:**
    *   Avoid jargon where possible.
    *   Use verbs that imply action and control (e.g., "Monetize," "Engage," "Scale").
    *   "No Vanity Metrics" – be specific (e.g., instead of "Grow fast," say "Increase ad revenue").

## 5. Key Homepage Modules (The Hybrid Approach)

### A. Hero: "The Living Canvas"
*   **Visual:** Full-screen video loop (abstract moving shapes or blurred lifestyle footage).
*   **Tech Overlay:** A subtle grid or data points tracking elements in the video (symbolizing AI analysis).
*   **Copy:** Short, punchy value prop. "Video that means business."

### B. Value Prop: "The Bento Grid"
*   A modular grid system.
*   **Module 1 (Tech):** A live graph showing revenue ticking up.
*   **Module 2 (Video):** A loop of a premium ad format.
*   **Module 3 (Human):** A portrait of a creator or client, smiling.
*   **Interaction:** Hovering over one module dims the others, focusing attention.

### C. Audience Switcher
*   A toggle that transforms the page content.
*   **State A (Advertisers):** Focus on ROI, Targeting, Reach.
*   **State B (Publishers):** Focus on Revenue, Player Tech, Stability.
*   **Animation:** The switch isn't instant; it "morphs" the grid.

### D. Trust Bar
*   Minimalist logos. Scrolling ticker. "Trusted by..."
